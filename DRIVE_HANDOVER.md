# Switch uploads to Alliance’s Google Drive

**Keep the current Google Sheet.** Its contents and ownership can stay as they are. The Alliance account needs **Editor** access so the form can save new records.

1. **Choose the folder.** Sign in to Alliance’s Google Drive. Create or open the folder for future uploads. Copy its link and share the folder with the people who need to open the files.
2. **Check the Sheet once.** Open the current index Sheet and click **Share**. If the Alliance email already shows **Editor**, you’re done with this step. Otherwise, add the email, choose **Editor**, and click **Send**.
3. **Start the switch.** Paste the prompt below into a new session in this repository. Fill in the email and folder link. When Google asks, sign in as the Alliance account and approve access.

> Follow DRIVE_HANDOVER.md. Switch future uploads to [Alliance email] using [Drive folder link]. Keep the current Sheet, keywords, existing files, and public form link. Back up the index and folder map, test the switch, publish, and verify that an Alliance user can upload, find, and open a file.
